# Connectly
### Connect. Chat. Share.

Connectly is a simple, WhatsApp-inspired messaging and file-sharing app that helps you connect your devices and share content with other people.

## Features

- **💬 Real-Time Messaging** — Send and receive text messages with connected contacts.
- **📁 File Sharing** — Transfer files directly between connected devices.
- **👥 Saved Contacts** — Save paired contacts and reconnect without repeating the pairing process.
- **🔗 Device Pairing** — Connect your devices or communicate with other people.
- **🕘 Chat History** — Keep your conversations saved on your device.
- **📱 Mobile-Friendly Design** — Enjoy a clean, familiar interface on phones and computers.
- **💾 Local Storage** — Keep your saved chats and contacts on your device.
- **🌐 Cross-Network Connections** — Connect across different networks when the connection and relay settings are configured correctly.
- **⚙️ Connection Settings** — Configure supported connection options to help devices connect across different networks.
- **📲 Installable App** — Install Connectly on a supported device for convenient access.

## Getting Started for the First Time

### 1. Open Connectly

Open Connectly in your browser on your computer or phone.

### 2. Set Up Your Connection

Open **Settings** and configure the available connection options if required. For connections across Wi-Fi and mobile data, your connection may need a compatible signaling service and TURN relay configuration.

### 3. Connect Your Devices

Follow the pairing instructions displayed in Connectly. Open the app on the other device and complete the connection process using the provided pairing method.

Keep both devices online while establishing the initial connection.

### 4. Save Your Contact

Once connected, save the contact if the option is available. You can then select the saved contact for future conversations without repeating the entire pairing process, provided the connection can be re-established.

### 5. Start Chatting

Open the contact's conversation and send your first message. You can also use the file-sharing option to select and send a file.

### 6. Check Your Chat History

Return to your chats to access conversations saved on the current device. Your locally saved history does not automatically transfer to another device.

### 7. Install Connectly (Optional)

If your browser offers an **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen** option, use it to access Connectly from your device's home screen.

## Important Notes

- Both devices generally need an internet connection to establish and use a remote connection.
- Cross-network connections depend on the signaling service, TURN configuration, network restrictions, and the other device being reachable.
- TURN relay settings help carry connection traffic when a direct connection is unavailable; they do not provide offline message delivery by themselves.
- Chat history and saved contacts are stored locally and may be lost if browser data is cleared or the app's local storage is removed.
- Messages are not guaranteed to arrive while a device is offline or the app is closed.
- Only share files and messages with people you trust.
- Keep connection credentials private, and avoid including private credentials in public backups.
